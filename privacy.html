Privacy

In short: MarkTML collects no data.

Last updated: 6 November 2026. Responsible: Andreas Stricker, marktml@e.mail.ch.

The app

MarkTML sends nothing over the internet. It has no account, no ads and no usage tracking, and it has no permission to access the network at all.

Your texts stay where you save them. If a file is in a cloud service such as iCloud Drive, that service’s terms apply. Settings and the folders you have granted access to are remembered only on your Mac.

Clicking a link in a text opens your browser or the app responsible for it.

App Store

If you download MarkTML from the App Store, Apple processes your data under its own terms. If you have agreed to share with app developers, Apple may provide crash reports and aggregated figures. These do not reveal your texts.

This website

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no third-party fonts or services. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub processes technical details such as your IP address to deliver and protect the site.

If you write to me

I use your message and address only to reply. You can ask me to delete them at any time.
